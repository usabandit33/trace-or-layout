Ask on the call

  1. Is the price a restripe of existing lines, or a new layout?
  2. Does the quote include the accessible-stall count and access-aisle width, or only paint over the old stalls?
  3. Are fire-lane words and curb paint included, or a separate line?
  4. Is sealcoating in this quote or a later visit?
