Trace the old lines, or lay out the lot?

Denver parking-lot paint quotes mix two jobs. One traces what is already there. The other measures a new layout, including accessible stalls and access aisles.

Ask before you book: Is this a restripe of the faded lines, or a new layout with stall count, access aisles, and fire-lane lettering written on the quote?

Property managers on public forums price striping by the line, the stall, or the linear foot — and a new layout is a different number from tracing what is already painted. Shops in Denver publish both services on their own sites.
