What to ask

Why the two quotes differ

Restriping follows lines that are already on the pavement. A new layout measures stalls, drive aisles, and accessible spaces before anyone paints. Striping crews on public forums describe new layouts as a higher per-stall price than a restripe of faded lines.

The U.S. Access Board’s restriping brief says that when a lot is restriped, accessible spaces must meet the 2010 Standards. The count is per lot, not per campus. At least one of every six accessible spaces must be van accessible. Car spaces in that guidance are at least 96 inches wide with a 60-inch access aisle. Tracing a too-narrow old stall does not answer that.

Questions

  • Is this price to trace the faded lines, or to lay out the lot again?
  • How many accessible stalls and van stalls are in the quote for this stall count?
  • Are access aisles and the ISA sign in the price?
  • Is fire-lane lettering included?
  • Does sealcoating have to cure before this paint, and is it a separate invoice?

Sources to read yourself: ada.gov parking, ADA restriping brief, layout vs restripe discussion. This page does not set a Denver price.
